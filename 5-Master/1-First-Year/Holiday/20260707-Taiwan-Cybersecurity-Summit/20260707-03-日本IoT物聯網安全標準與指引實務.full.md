I am a little too old to be an AI native, but I am still actively working in the PSIRT environment and supporting many global manufacturers in establishing their product security capabilities. Briefly introducing GMO Cybersecurity by Ierae: we are one of the largest and leading cybersecurity companies in Japan. （雖然我年紀稍微大了一點，不是 AI 原生世代，但我至今仍深耕於 PSIRT 實務圈，協助許多國際製造大廠從零建立其產品資安體系。今天我也帶來了兩本我撰寫的書籍，不過是日文版，大家可能需要翻譯工具輔助。簡單介紹一下 GMO Cybersecurity by Ierae：我們是日本規模最大、最前端的資安公司之一，旗下編制有 160 至 170 位頂尖的內部資安研究員與白帽專家。業務涵蓋弱點評估、SOC 監控、法規合規顧問，以及為日本防衛省、警察大學提供專業防禦培訓。目前我們更積極拓展至太空衛星資安領域，並已與臺灣的臺北科技大學簽署了合作備忘錄。）

Now, moving into product security. What is product security? It is somewhat hard to define, but I firmly believe that product security is fundamentally a "Product Safety" issue rooted in cybersecurity risk. It is not merely an IT security concern; it is about safety—preventing physical damage, service interruption, and inconvenience for users, including both consumers and enterprise clients. Therefore, product security must be treated as product quality rather than internal operational security.

（現在進入今天的主題：究竟什麼是「產品資安（Product Security）」？這個詞看似有些抽象，但我深信：**產品資安本質上就是基於網路安全風險的「產品安全（Product Safety）」問題**！它絕非單純的企業內部 IT 防護，而是關於人身與運行安全的課題——防止對終端消費者與企業用戶造成人身損害、嚴重不便或財產損失。因此，產品資安必須被視為「產品品質管制」的核心一環，而不僅僅是企業內部的營運安全防護。）

## 🚗 歷史借鏡與法規革命：從車聯網、物聯網到歐盟 CRA

**【伊豆倉 憲 博士】**：There are many historical incidents that catalyzed this regulatory shift. In the automotive industry, a monumental incident occurred and was revealed at Black Hat 2015.

**【伊豆倉 憲 博士】**：Although no casualties were involved, researchers demonstrated that a vehicle could be remotely controlled—steering, braking, and transmission—without any physical modification to the car.

**【伊豆倉 憲 博士】**：This sent shockwaves throughout the global automotive industry.

**【伊豆倉 憲 博士】**：It resulted in massive, costly recalls to patch the firmware without warranty support, directly giving birth to automotive cybersecurity regulations such as UN R155 and ISO/SAE 21434.

（回顧歷史，有許多重大資安事件催生了這場全球法規革命。在汽車工業領域，最震撼的莫過於 2015 年在 Black Hat 大會上發表的車聯網入侵研究。雖然當時並未造成實質傷亡，但研究人員證實了無須對車輛進行任何硬體改裝，即可透過遠端無線訊號完全接管汽車的轉向、煞車與油門。這對全球汽車製造業帶來了毀滅性的衝擊，迫使車廠展開極為昂貴的全面召回行動，也直接催生了聯合國 UN R155 與 ISO/SAE 21434 等全球車輛網路安全強制法規。）

Another well-known case is the Mirai botnet, which infected millions of IoT devices worldwide and led countries like the US, UK, and Japan to establish foundational baseline security rules for IoT. In the medical industry, implanted pacemakers were discovered to have vulnerabilities where their operational configurations could be manipulated via Bluetooth from outside the patient's body. This was a direct life-or-death issue for patients. Consequently, the game has changed: manufacturers who were traditionally sensitive only to physical safety must now recognize that products are increasingly controlled by software, making cyber risk an inherent safety risk.

（另一個廣為人知的案例是 Mirai 殭屍網路，它感染了全球數百萬台物聯網攝影機與路由器，迫使美、英、日等國開始針對一般消費型 IoT 制定基礎安全基準。在醫療器材領域，心律調節器（Pacemaker）被發現存在可透過體外藍牙通訊被未授權竄改參數的重大漏洞，這直接關乎病患的生命安全！因此，產業的遊戲規則已經徹底翻轉：過去製造業只對物理機械的安全規格極度敏感，但如今所有產品的核心功能皆由軟體代碼驅動，網路資安風險已正式成為攸關生死的核心安全指標。）

In 2024, the European Union updated the Product Liability Directive (PLD). Crucially, the EU expanded the scope of product liability from tangible physical goods to include "software itself". This delivered a monumental message to global manufacturers: software quality and security assurance are legally binding product liabilities. Furthermore, a wave of stringent regulations is arriving, including the Radio Equipment Directive (RED), Machinery Regulation, Data Act, AI Act, and prominently, the Cyber Resilience Act (CRA).

（在 2024 年，歐盟正式修訂了《產品責任指令》（Product Liability Directive, PLD）。最關鍵的變革在於，歐盟將產品責任的法律範疇從過往的「有形實體硬體」正式擴大納入「**軟體本身**」！這向全球製造業傳遞了一個不容忽視的強烈訊號：軟體品質保證與資安防護已成為具備法律效力的產品責任。與此同時，包括無線電設備指令（RED）、機械法規、資料法案、AI 法案，以及最為核心的《網路韌性法案》（CRA），正排山倒海而來。）

## ⚖️ 歐盟網路韌性法案 (CRA) 核心衝擊：24 小時通報與 2027 CE 認證大限

**【伊豆倉 憲 博士】**：What does the EU CRA require from manufacturers? The CRA regulates over one hundred specific items, but the core requirements center around:
1.

**【伊豆倉 憲 博士】**：**Security by Design**: Products must be designed based on risk assessments, embedding countermeasures and mitigation from the earliest architectural phase;
2.

**【伊豆倉 憲 博士】**：**Technical Documentation**: Manufacturers must maintain comprehensive evidence and documentation covering the pre-market development lifecycle;
3.

**【伊豆倉 憲 博士】**：**Regulatory Authority Engagement**: Companies must establish clear processes to interface with EU authorities and provide conformity evidence upon request;
4.

**【伊豆倉 憲 博士】**：**Vulnerability and Incident Handling**: Manufacturers must establish dedicated capabilities to handle reported vulnerabilities, issue security patches, and maintain post-market security throughout the product lifecycle.

（歐盟《網路韌性法案》（CRA）究竟對製造業者提出了哪些強制要求？CRA 在法案中規範了上百項具體要求，但核心聚焦於四大面向：
1. **安全設計（Security by Design）**：產品必須基於風險評估從架構設計之初就內建防禦機制，嚴格落實威脅消除；
2. **技術文件與證據保存**：製造商必須完整保留上市前各個研發階段的合規技術文檔與客觀證據；
3. **主管機關對接能力**：企業必須具備與歐盟各國主管機關正式對接的法規流程，隨時準備接受抽查並提供合規證據；
4. **弱點管理與事件應變**：製造商必須具備完善的弱點通報處理與修補發布能量，並在產品整個生命週期內持續提供安全性維護。）

Is anyone here a manufacturer exporting digital products to the European Union? If you sell products with digital elements in Europe, you have very little time left. Article 14 of the CRA mandates that if you discover an actively exploited vulnerability or a severe security incident in your product, you must report it to the designated EU CSIRT within **24 hours**! This reporting mandate becomes effective in just a couple of months.

（在座有製造業者將含數位元素的產品外銷至歐盟市場的嗎？如果有的話，留給各位的準備時間已經非常緊迫了！CRA 第 14 條（Article 14）明確規定：一旦在市售產品中發現被積極利用的重大弱點或發生嚴重資安事件，**製造商必須在 24 小時之內向歐盟指定的 CSIRT 組織正式通報**！這項通報法規即將在短短兩個月內正式生效。）

Furthermore, by **December 2027**, no manufacturer can sell or export products with digital elements into the EU market without full compliance with CRA requirements and possessing the valid CE marking! If your product arrives at a European seaport or airport without the proper conformity documentation, it will be refused customs clearance. Products are classified into Default, Important Class I (such as OS, routers, switches), Important Class II (firewalls, hypervisors), and Critical (smart meters, cryptographic chips), with higher tiers requiring mandatory third-party audits or EUCC common criteria certifications.

（更嚴峻的是，到了 **2027 年 12 月**，任何未全面符合 CRA 規範、未取得合規 CE 標誌的數位產品，將**全面禁止在歐盟境內銷售或進口**！屆時若您的產品抵達歐洲海港或機場，缺乏合格的技術文檔與 CE 標誌，海關將直接拒絕通關。法案將產品劃分為一般數位產品、重要 Class I（如作業系統、路由器、交換器）、重要 Class II（防火牆、虛擬化監控器）以及關鍵等級（如智慧電表、安全加密晶片），較高風險級別甚至強制要求第三方評估機構進行嚴格查核或取得 EUCC 共同準則認證。）

## 🤖 PSIRT 2.0 轉型與 AI 人機在環 (Human-in-the-Loop) 治理

**【伊豆倉 憲 博士】**：To manage these responsibilities, manufacturers establish a PSIRT (Product Security Incident Response Team).

**【伊豆倉 憲 博士】**：However, our 2023 survey across Japanese manufacturer PSIRTs revealed that only 61% have dedicated, full-time personnel; the majority operate with part-time, ad-hoc staff under severe budget constraints.

**【伊豆倉 憲 博士】**：Furthermore, very few teams currently utilize AI or machine learning effectively.

（為了承擔這些法規與維運責任，製造業者通常會設立 PSIRT（產品安全事件應變小組）。然而根據我們在 2023 年針對日本企業 PSIRT 進行的深度調查發現，僅有 61% 的團隊配置有全職員工，絕大多數都處於兼職兼辦、預算極度拮据的狀態。更甚者，極少數團隊有能力真正導入 AI 或機器學習工具來減輕負擔。）

To overcome this resource scarcity, we are advocating two fundamental shifts. First, shifting from siloed, proprietary PSIRTs to an **Organic Platform-based PSIRT**, sharing common operational infrastructure across business units. Second, introducing **AI-assisted automation with a strict Human-in-the-Loop architecture**.

（為了解決資源匱乏的困境，我們倡導兩大根本性轉革：第一，從各部門閉門造車、各自為政的傳統模式，轉型為**橫向平台化的 PSIRT（Platform-based PSIRT）**，在企業各事業群之間共享通用的安全維運基礎設施；第二，**全面導入 AI 輔助自動化，但嚴格維持「Human-in-the-Loop（人機在環）」架構**。）

While AI can automate vulnerability scanning, advisory drafting, and text-based regulatory matching, human judgment must remain strictly at critical decision points:
1. **Corporate-Level Risk Assessment**: Determining whether a vulnerability poses an existential business risk;
2. **Vendor and Supply Chain Communications**: Managing negotiations, budgets, and partner liability;
3. **Regulatory Contextual Interpretation**: Understanding the underlying intent behind international legal mandates, which LLMs cannot fully grasp.

My concluding message: If you don't have a PSIRT, start now. If your PSIRT is struggling with resources, leverage AI—but never rely 100% on AI. Keep human decision-making at the center of the design to achieve sustainable, resilient product security throughout the entire lifecycle. Thank you very much!

（儘管生成式 AI 能夠自動執行弱點情報檢索、初步安全通告草擬與法規條文比對，但在以下關鍵節點，**必須嚴格保留人類專家的裁決權**：
1. **企業層級風險定性**：判定特定弱點是否會對公司商譽或營運造成致命衝擊；
2. **供應鏈與供應商關係維繫**：牽涉商業談判、合約預算與跨國夥伴責任歸屬；
3. **法規底層意涵的實質解讀**：理解國際法規背後真正的監管初衷，而非僅止於大模型表面字詞的比對。

我最後想傳達給各位的訊息：如果您公司尚未建立 PSIRT，請立即著手建立；如果您現有的 PSIRT 正面臨資源瓶頸，請善用 AI 賦能——但切記絕不能 100% 依賴 AI！在整個流程核心嚴格維持人類決策（Human in the loop），我們才能在產品整個生命週期中實現兼具效率與韌性的產品安全防護。非常感謝大家！）

在推動合規的過程中，我們發現市面上現成的大型資安工具大多只針對 IT 系統網路安全，專門針對製造業產線與嵌入式裝置的工具極其有限。因此，松下內部自行研發了三套專屬的產品資安管理系統，落實於全集團的開發流程之中……
